bash install_plane_desklet.sh

- Starting out: your unique-plane history is kept, but the graph starts fresh. It begins with a dot on the right, and lines appear as more readings arrive. It takes 24 hours to span the full width.

- Updates: it adds a point about every 5 minutes when your station responds, plus one when the desklet loads. Points older than 24 hours drop off, and saved points survive restarts.

- Scale: the vertical scale adjusts to the highest tracked count on the graph, shown above it as scale 0 to N. The middle grid line is half that number, and the vertical lines are 6 hours apart.

- Gaps: breaks in the readings longer than 7½ minutes are left blank, such as when your computer is asleep. A single missed reading can leave a gap too.
